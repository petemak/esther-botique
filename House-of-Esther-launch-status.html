What is working today

  • The store is online and open to the public.
  • You can sign in to the admin area and add products one by one, or in bulk from a spreadsheet (CSV) together with the photos.
  • Product photos are saved safely in cloud storage and show on the store.
  • Customers can browse, add items to the cart and place an order.
  • Delivery is available countrywide, with a delivery fee that depends on the customer's county.
  • You can add and manage team members (owner and staff) from the admin area.

How customers pay

Pay on delivery only. The customer pays the rider when the order arrives, by M-Pesa or in cash. Customers are not charged online, so there is nothing to reconcile before an order is delivered.

Paying with M-Pesa on the website (an automatic payment prompt on the customer's phone) is built but switched off. It can be turned on later once you have a Safaricom M-Pesa business account set up.

Your next steps

  1. Add your products. One at a time: open Add product (steps in the guide). Many at once: use the Bulk import page to upload a spreadsheet and photos together (steps in the guide). New products are saved as drafts.
  2. Publish them. Customers only see published products. Open your Products list to publish; several can be published at once.
  3. Review the legal pages. The Terms, Privacy and Returns pages hold placeholder wording. Check and edit them on the Legal documents page (see the guide) before you promote the store.
  4. Check the delivery fees customers see on the Delivery & Returns page. If any should change, let us know.
  5. When orders arrive, manage them on the Orders page (see the guide) and keep stock correct on the Inventory page (see the guide).
